06 · RADIO INTEGRATION

The only AI assistant that talks to your radio.

Roger·AI's expansion header exposes RADIO_PTT and RADIO_COR signals — giving it the ability to interface with any amateur or professional radio transceiver. AI understands the transmission, responds on air, and logs everything.

RADIO_PTTOutput → triggers radio transmit relay
RADIO_CORInput ← Carrier Operated Relay (channel open detect)
