DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

UX Principles

Every design decision in TDC Turbo serves one goal: the pilot should never think about the board during flight.

🧠

Zero Cognitive Load

One button. Press = turbo. Release = normal. No menus, no modes, no configuration mid-flight.

⚡

Instant Feedback

Strobe flash confirms turbo is active. Visible to pilot (through camera) and spectators on the ground.

🛡️

Fail-Safe Default

Board boots in bypass mode. If MCU crashes, resets, or loses power — bypass stays ON. Quad keeps flying.

📊

Transparent Monitoring

App shows real-time cell voltages. Pilot trusts the system because they can verify every number.

👻

Invisible When Not Needed

During normal flight, the board is just a wire. Zero added resistance. Zero latency. Board weighs less than 8 grams.

🔧

Quick Pit Turnaround

Swap main pack, keep turbo pack. 45-second pit stop. Same turnaround as every other pilot.

🎪

Social Proof & Spectacle

The strobe flash creates a MOMENT. Announcers comment. Crowds react. It's a feature, not just an indicator.

🤝

Progressive Trust

Test on bench (button + app), then in practice, then in competition. Trust builds before the stakes are high.
