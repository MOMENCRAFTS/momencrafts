The Innovation

Protect. Play. Pocket.

Edge Tack merges two essential accessories into one — a premium screen protector with collapsible pneumatic trigger buttons built into the frame edges.

🛡

Protect

9H tempered glass screen protector. Full-coverage edge-to-edge protection. The foundation you already need — now with superpowers.

🎮

Play

L1/R1-style pneumatic trigger buttons deploy from the frame in under 1 second. Tactile click feedback. Console-grade precision on your phone.

Pocket

Triggers fold completely flat. Zero bulk. Fits in any case. Your phone looks and feels exactly like it does with a normal screen protector.
